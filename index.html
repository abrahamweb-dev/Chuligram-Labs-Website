Chuligram at a glance

What is Chuligram?
Chuligram is a communication and social platform combining messaging, large communities, channels, social discovery, short-form video, calls, creator experiences and intelligent technology.
Why combine messaging and social media?
People currently switch between different apps for private conversations, communities, social posts, short videos, creators and AI. Chuligram's goal is to connect these experiences so users can move between them naturally from one account and one ecosystem.
Is Chuligram trying to replace Telegram, Facebook and TikTok?
The product direction is to combine useful categories of experience found across major platforms while building Chuligram's own identity, interface, technology and community model. It is not intended to be a literal copy of another service.
Do I need separate apps for messaging, social content and short videos?
The long-term Chuligram vision is no. The platform is being designed so messaging, communities, social discovery, short-form video and AI can live together as connected parts of the same ecosystem.

Messaging

What can I send in Chuligram?
The messaging experience is designed for text, photos, videos, files, voice messages, reactions, replies, forwarding and other everyday communication tools.
Can I edit or delete messages?
Message management is part of the platform experience, including editing and deletion flows where supported by the current application version and conversation permissions.
Can I reply, react, copy and forward messages?
Yes, these are core messaging interactions planned and supported across the Chuligram messaging experience, with permissions and privacy rules applied where appropriate.
Can I send large files?
File sharing is part of Chuligram's communication layer. Actual size limits depend on the deployed storage and backend configuration, so limits should only be advertised after they are finalized and tested.

Groups & communities

How large can a Chuligram group become?
Chuligram is designed with a target of supporting communities of up to 400,000 members. This is a platform target, not a claim that every current deployment has already been load-tested at that scale.
What features are planned for large groups?
Replies, mentions, hashtags, reactions, smart notifications, pinned messages, administrator controls, moderation, anti-spam tools, group permissions, topics and public-community discovery are part of the product direction.
Can communities be public or private?
Yes. Chuligram's community model is intended to support different privacy and discovery levels so people can create spaces for friends, schools, organizations, interests and public communities.

Can I post photos and videos?
Media-rich social publishing is part of the Chuligram experience, with support designed for posts, visual content and short-form video.
Will Chuligram have a TikTok-style short-video feed?
Yes, short-form video is part of the product direction, with an emphasis on discovery, creators, trends, entertainment and community engagement. Recommendation systems and safety controls will need careful development before being treated as final.

Getting Started

The basics of Chuligram and what the platform is designed to do.

Chuligram is a communication and social platform designed to bring messaging, communities, channels, social discovery, short-form video, creator experiences and intelligent technology together in one place.

You can use Chuligram to send messages, share photos, videos and files, make calls, participate in private conversations, create communities, publish through channels, discover content and connect with other people.

Messages and conversations

Chuligram provides the everyday communication tools expected from a modern messaging platform, including replies, reactions, forwarding, editing, deletion, media sharing, file sharing, voice messages, notifications and search.

Groups and communities

Groups are designed for large communities, with a product target of supporting communities of up to 400,000 members. Community features include replies, mentions, hashtags, reactions, pinned messages, permissions, moderation, anti-spam protection and topics.

About the 400,000-member target: reaching and reliably operating at that scale requires serious backend capacity, database design, realtime infrastructure, caching, rate limiting, moderation and load testing. The figure is a platform target, not a claim that every current deployment has already been load-tested at that size.

Channels and social discovery

Channels are designed for creators, organizations, businesses and communities that want to publish to an audience. The social side of Chuligram is intended to help people discover posts, creators, communities, photos, videos and short-form content.

Chuligram AI

Chuligram AI is being developed as a dedicated AI platform rather than simply a chatbot attached to messaging. Its direction includes conversation, reasoning, coding, research, web and knowledge tools, file analysis, image understanding, voice, memory, model routing and agent workflows.

Privacy and security

Privacy is a core design goal. Users can control important account and communication settings, including who can contact them, call them and see selected profile information.

Advanced cryptographic features such as end-to-end encrypted conversations should only be described as fully available after their underlying architecture has been implemented, tested and independently reviewed.

In short: Chuligram is being built as a place where people can talk, build communities, share, discover, create and work with AI.
